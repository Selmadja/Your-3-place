your 3ʳᵈ place

C

The third place is a social space outside the home (the first place) and workplace (the second place).
It was first introduced by Ray Oldenburg in his book "In The Great Good Place"
Our platform "your 3ʳᵈ place " take you to discover different types of third places available in your city where people can meet, talk, relax, and build relationships and create connection, friendship, belonging, and a stronger public life.
